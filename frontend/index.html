About IndicScript

IndicScript is an advanced manuscript digitization tool designed to bridge the gap between ancient Indian heritage and modern technology. Using specialized AI models tailored for Devanagari and regional scripts, we turn historical handwritten manuscripts into searchable, editable digital archives.

How It Works

1. Upload

Upload a clean photo, scan, or PDF of your historical or handwritten manuscript document.

2. Process & OCR

Select your source and target languages. Our pipeline analyzes characters and extracts the text accurately.

3. Translate & Export

Review the transcription, view the instant translation tab, and download your text file with one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are supported?

Currently, IndicScript supports high-resolution PDF, PNG, and JPG files.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on the clarity of the manuscript handwriting, but our models are specifically optimized for historical Indic scripts.

Can I edit the extracted text?

Yes! Both the transcription and translation output panels feature editable text areas so you can correct any OCR discrepancies on the fly.
